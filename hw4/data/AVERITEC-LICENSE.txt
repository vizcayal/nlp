AVeriTeC course subset

Source: AVeriTeC: A Dataset for Real-world Claim Verification with Evidence
from the Web, Michael Schlichtkrull, Zhijiang Guo, and Andreas Vlachos (2023).
Dataset: https://fever.ai/dataset/averitec.html
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13117
Repository: https://github.com/MichSchli/AVeriTeC

The source dataset is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Course adaptation: selected Supported/Refuted claims, JSONL field projection,
and staff-authored Part 3 candidate questions and keys. Original retained claims,
labels, and question-answer evidence text are preserved. Source provenance and
staff-held evaluation records are distributed separately to course staff.
